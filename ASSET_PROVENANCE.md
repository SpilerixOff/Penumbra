## Meshy-generated props

The maintainer confirmed on 2026-10-21 that the twelve props below were generated by them using a paid Meshy account. They are included as project-owned assets under the repository's MIT license, not as assets copied from Meshy's public community catalogue.

The corresponding OBJ files are in `src/main/resources/assets/penumbra/meshes/`, with textures under `src/main/resources/assets/penumbra/textures/entity/prop/`:

`borne`, `cairn`, `campement`, `chaise`, `croix`, `effigie`, `miroir`, `nid`, `porte`, `poupee`, `souche`, `theodolite`.

Prompts are documented in [PROMPTS_PROPS.md](docs/PROMPTS_PROPS.md). The import tools normalize and triangulate geometry and resize textures for the game. Meshy is credited here as the generation tool, not as a project sponsor. See [Meshy's explanation of paid-plan asset ownership](https://help.meshy.ai/en/articles/10137554-what-is-the-ownership-of-the-generated-models).

The separate `effigy.obj` is generated procedurally by `tools/gen_effigy_mesh.py`.

## Images and sound

- Project documentation records original AI-assisted artwork and procedurally generated textures. Generation and conversion scripts are in `tools/`, with available reference images in `tools/image_sources/`.
- Some historical scripts refer to an untracked `ai/` directory. Those optional source images are not required to compile or play: the processed game resources are included in `src/main/resources/`. The scripts' historical regeneration steps require supplying the corresponding original references.
- Sound synthesis and the original music track are documented in [SOUNDS.md](docs/SOUNDS.md) and their generation scripts. Building the mod does not run these Python tools or require an AI service.
- [ASSETS.md](docs/ASSETS.md) documents resource dimensions, origins and transformations. Historical descriptions there may refer to earlier versions.
